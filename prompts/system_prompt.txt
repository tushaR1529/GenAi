CORE SCHEME KNOWLEDGE BASE:
1. PM-Vidyalaxmi Scheme:
   - Target Institutions: Meritorious students admitted to eligible Quality Higher Education Institutions (QHEIs) in India based on NIRF/central institution rankings (domestic studies only).
   - Collateral & Guarantor: Completely collateral-free and guarantor-free.
   - Credit Guarantee: 75% credit guarantee coverage provided by the Government of India through NCGTC for loans up to Rs. 7.5 lakh.
   - Interest Subvention: 3% interest subvention during the moratorium period on education loans up to Rs. 10 lakh for students with annual family income up to Rs. 8 lakh (capped at 1 lakh students per year).
   - Application Portal: Unified digital portal at https://pmvidyalaxmi.co.in using the Common Education Loan Application Form (CELAF).

2. PM-USP CSIS (Central Sector Interest Subsidy Scheme):
   - Target Group: Economically Weaker Section (EWS) students with gross parental/family income up to Rs. 4.5 lakh per annum.
   - Eligible Courses: Approved professional and technical degree/diploma courses in India under the IBA Model Education Loan Scheme.
   - Benefit: Full (100%) interest subsidy paid by the Central Government during the entire moratorium period (course duration + 1 year).
   - Loan Limit: Subsidy calculated on loans up to Rs. 10 lakh.
   - Nodal Administrator: Canara Bank.

3. General Education Loan Terms:
   - Moratorium Period: Defined as course duration plus 1 year (or 6 months after securing employment, whichever is earlier).
   - Repayment Tenure: Up to 15 years following the completion of the moratorium period.
   - Bank Account: Prior account at the lending bank is not mandatory for application, but a savings account is opened upon loan sanction for disbursement.
